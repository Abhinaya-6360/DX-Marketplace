Frequently asked

Questions, answered

Do clients need to create an account to book?

No. Clients open your booking link, pick a service and time, and confirm — no sign-up or app download required on their end.

Which calendars does Bookings sync with?

Google Calendar, Microsoft Outlook/365, and Zoho Calendar all sync two-way, so a booking blocks the slot everywhere instantly.

Can I collect payment when someone books?

Yes, on Premium and above. Connect Stripe, PayPal, Square, or Razorpay to charge a deposit or the full amount at booking time.

Is there a limit on the number of bookings?

No plan — including Free — caps the number of appointments you can take. You only pay per staff member added.

Can I cancel or change plans later?

Yes, you can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel from your billing settings at any time, effective at the end of the current cycle.
